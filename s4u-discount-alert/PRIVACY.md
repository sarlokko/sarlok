# Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

S4U Discount Alert is a private, non-commercial Reddit Devvit application used
by its owner to receive notifications about public S4U watch-face discounts.

## Data accessed

The application reads public Reddit post and comment content from its configured
developer account and public subreddits. It uses only the content, timestamps,
links, post IDs, subreddit names, and public author names needed to identify
qualifying offers.

## Data use and retention

Qualifying offer details are sent in a private message to the configured Reddit
account. Reddit post IDs that have been notified are retained in Devvit Redis
for 45 days solely to prevent duplicate notifications. No user profiles or
private Reddit data are collected, and Reddit data is not sold, shared, used for
advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

## Third-party services

The application uses Reddit Devvit for execution, data access, storage, and
private-message delivery. Reddit's privacy policy applies to those services.

## Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the source repository that publishes
this policy.
